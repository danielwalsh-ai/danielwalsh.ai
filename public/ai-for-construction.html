AI for construction companies

For builders and contractors whose office has grown faster than their revenue. I run a construction business myself, so I know exactly which paperwork eats your week and what AI can honestly do about it.

The problem is never the site. It's the office.

The jobs get done. The problem is everything wrapped around them: quotes that take evenings to price, valuations and applications for payment, RAMS and compliance paperwork, subcontractor invoices and CIS, retentions nobody chases, and job costs you only see clearly months after the job's finished. The office team grows, the directors work weekends, and admin quietly becomes the ceiling on how much work you can take on.

Hiring more office staff is the expensive answer. Most of that work follows the same steps every time, and work that follows the same steps every time is exactly what AI is good at.

What AI actually does inside a construction firm

  • Quoting and take-offs. Drawings, spec documents and supplier price lists read automatically, so a first-pass estimate takes minutes instead of an evening.
  • Job costing you can see. Labour, materials and plant against each job, live, not six weeks later in the accounts. You find the losing job while you can still do something about it.
  • Subcontractor admin. Invoices checked against orders, CIS handled, payment and deduction statements generated and sent without anyone retyping anything.
  • Site reports and compliance. Voice notes from site turned into daily diaries, RAMS drafted from templates, evidence filed where you can find it when it matters.
  • Chasing money. Applications, retentions and overdue accounts tracked and chased politely and relentlessly, so cash stops leaking through politeness.

None of this is a chatbot on your website. It's back-office machinery that runs every day whether you're in or not.
